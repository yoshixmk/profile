Full-stack developer with expertise in modern web technologies and a passion for building scalable, performant applications. Experienced in TypeScript, React, Next.js, and cloud infrastructure.

- **Frameworks**: Next.js 15, React, Vue 3

- **Animation**: Framer Motion, tsparticles
