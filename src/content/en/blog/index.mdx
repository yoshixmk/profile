## Profile
Born in 1992 in Okayama Prefecture, Japan.
After graduating from a science-focused high school,
studied programming at Chugoku Vocational Ability Development College. There, engaged in robot development using embedded technology and served as president of the robot competition club, while witnessing the amazing spread of web technology.

After moving to Tokyo, worked as a Backend/SRE engineer at USERBASE Inc. (2016-2019).

From 2019, worked as a full-stack engineer at Astamuse Co., Ltd. Responsible for idea sheet/business evaluation features and authentication infrastructure replacement (2019-2022).

From 2022, working as a full-stack engineer (backend-focused) at Money Forward, Inc. Responsible for mortgage loan deduction/hometown tax features, electronic filing systems, document AI-OCR features, BFF construction, etc. (2022-)

Started career as a backend engineer and SRE, now working full-stack with a focus on backend.
